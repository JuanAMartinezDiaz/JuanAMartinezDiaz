# Decision memo — Hold CandidateRank live deployment

**To:** HarborBridge Executive Risk Committee (fictional)  
**From:** AI Risk Review function (scenario role)  
**Decision reference:** DEC-003  
**Status:** worked recommendation; no actual committee approval

CandidateRank should not screen or rank real applicants at this stage. Permit a synthetic evaluation in an isolated environment while HR retains the existing manual process.

The proposed benefit is faster screening. No evidence yet shows that the benefit outweighs hiring errors, unequal access to opportunities, or an inability to explain decisions. In the invented measurement example, selection rates differ by 24 percentage points. That signal requires investigation; it is not proof of the cause. Vendor accuracy claims cannot resolve it.

The assessment identifies one critical risk and six high risks. All current residual scores remain unchanged because the proposed controls lack operating evidence. Treating planned controls as proven would understate exposure.

**Authorize now:** a 30-day synthetic evaluation with version-pinned software, no real CVs, no outbound decision actions, and an explicit stop owner in HR. This is a proposed internal time box, not an approved budget or a legal grace period.

**Require before reconsideration:** independent validation of job relevance and subgroup behavior; privacy and security evidence; meaningful reviewer challenge; version and logging controls; applicable provider documentation; and a legally reviewed deployment scope. The review must also evaluate the simpler alternative of extraction without ranking.

HR owns the business case and remediation. Second-line AI risk independently challenges the evidence and records unresolved objections. Legal determines applicability. The executive committee decides within its approved risk appetite; it cannot waive law. Internal audit does not own release approval.

If the vendor cannot provide sufficient evidence within the evaluation time box, end the pilot and select an alternative. Success is a defensible decision and reduced exposure, not a predetermined approval.

**Follow-up:** HR returns with C1–C8 evidence from the assessment, unresolved limitations, actual evaluation cost, and a comparison against manual review. No customer or applicant impact, savings, or control effectiveness is claimed in this portfolio.
