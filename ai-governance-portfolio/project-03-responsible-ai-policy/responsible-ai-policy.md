# Responsible AI policy — HarborBridge

**Policy HB-AI-01 v1.0 · synthetic design · proposed owner: Chief Risk Officer.** Proposed annual review plus event-driven review. No board adoption or signatures are claimed.

## Scope and requirements

All business units must register AI before procurement, development, pilot access or material modification. The accountable business executive remains responsible for outcomes even where a vendor supplies the system. Staff must not place confidential data in unapproved AI tools.

| Commitment | Required process | Evidence / accountable role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lawful use | Legal scope and prohibition review before pilot | Applicability record / business owner with Legal review |
| Fair treatment | Assess affected people, accommodations and unequal error burdens | Impact assessment and validation report / business owner |
| Reliable outputs | Define intended purpose, failure conditions and measurable acceptance | Versioned test plan and independent findings / engineering owner |
| Effective oversight | Give trained people time, information and authority to disagree and stop use | Exercise results, permissions and override record / operations owner |
| Protected data | Minimize collection; enforce access and approved vendor use | Data flow, access tests and retention decision / data owner |
| Traceable action | Correlate inputs, versions, outputs, human decisions and execution | Reconstructable evidence record / platform owner |
| Controlled change | Reassess material changes before release | Change impact and renewed approval / business owner |
| Remediation | Stop harmful use, investigate and support affected people | Incident and redress records / incident commander |

## Authority boundaries

An AI-generated recommendation is not an authorization. Consequential actions require a separately enforced permission boundary and accountable human approval where policy requires it. Agents may not modify their own permissions, approve their own exceptions, or decide which evidence to delete. Execution rights must be limited by action, resource, amount, duration and delegating identity where relevant. This is a control requirement; implementation is not demonstrated by this document.

## Proportional review and exceptions

Low internal-risk uses may receive delegated approval after basic checks. High internal-risk or legally high-risk uses require independent validation and enhanced committee review. Uncertain classification goes to Legal and risk fact-finding. Prohibited use is rejected.

Exceptions must name the requirement, reason, compensating control, owner, expiration, affected systems, monitoring and rollback. Maximum proposed duration is 30 days, after which access expires unless explicitly renewed. Legal prohibitions, missing required approvals and unresolved critical risk are not exception-eligible. Commercial urgency is not a compensating control.

## Oversight and enforcement

Business owners attest quarterly. The governance office reports overdue reviews, unknown systems, expired exceptions, unresolved validation findings, incidents, and unauthorized actions monthly. A missing report is treated as missing evidence, not a zero-risk observation. Security may suspend access for an urgent threat; resumption follows the documented release gate.

Training must match roles: operators practice overrides, executives review risk acceptance, procurement checks supplier evidence, and developers practice authority limits. Attendance alone does not establish competence; scenario exercises are required. Third-party contracts must address change notice, incident cooperation, data use, evidence access and exit support.
