## Reporting decision log — worked disposition

**Facts at first hour:** possible systematic credit harm; causal link unconfirmed; no death or critical-infrastructure disruption described; affected-person count unresolved. **Disposition:** escalate immediately for Legal's assessment, preserve the earliest awareness time, and prepare a preliminary notice packet. Do not assert a completed filing or conclude “not reportable” from incomplete facts.

For applicable high-risk use, Article 26 distinguishes deployer escalation from provider reporting. Article 73 sets provider reporting requirements: generally immediate reporting once the causal link or reasonable likelihood is established, with a 15-day outer limit; specified widespread/infrastructure cases have a two-day limit, and death cases a ten-day limit. These are outer limits, not waiting periods. An incomplete initial report may be followed by a complete one. Review sectoral overlaps and the provider-unreachable case separately.

Before sending, the reporting owner records the applicable law and version, statutory trigger, awareness time, actual jurisdiction and authority, deadline calculation, responsible sender, facts versus uncertainty, and next update. GDPR and financial-sector obligations require separate assessment; this exercise invents neither a regulator address nor a universal deadline.

No regulator, vendor or customer has been contacted for this portfolio exercise.
