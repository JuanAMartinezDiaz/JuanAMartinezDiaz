# 02 — Risk management summary

**Synthetic deployer assessment and provider evidence request.** The provider should supply its lifecycle risk-management evidence; HarborBridge retains its own integration and use-context assessment. Article 9 is the principal provider risk-management reference.

R1–R8 from Project 2 remain relevant to HB-011. No score is reduced merely because the system is a replacement. Current evidence does not support live use. The central concern is unjustified exclusion from job opportunities combined with weak reviewer challenge and missing traceability.

| Treatment | Design decision | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Avoid | Remove biometric and social-media inference | Verified input contract and negative tests |
| Reduce | Source-linked assessments and reviewer-first scoring | Explanation checks and oversight exercises |
| Reduce | Isolated data and action permissions | Access tests and blocked unauthorized actions |
| Transfer contractual duties | Require vendor change/incident cooperation | Executed contract and exercised contact path; risk remains with accountable roles |
| Accept within appetite | Only bounded, evidenced residual issues | Named authorized acceptance, scope and expiry |

Risk log updates at design, evaluation, deployment, incident, material change and retirement. Validation must test foreseeable misuse, language variation, incomplete CVs, accommodations, malicious document content, and reviewer behavior. Business owners must consider a simpler non-ranking alternative and record why AI is appropriate.

Decision DEC-011: hold live deployment. Synthetic evaluation may be proposed under Project 3's controls. No actual committee approval exists. The release authority must see independently reviewed evidence, unresolved findings and rollback readiness; it cannot substitute a signed memo for missing tests.
