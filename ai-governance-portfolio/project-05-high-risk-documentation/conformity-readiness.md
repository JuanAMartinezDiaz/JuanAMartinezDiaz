## Provider and deployer responsibilities

The provider owns its technical documentation and applicable conformity assessment. For the assumed standalone recruitment use under Annex III point 4, Article 43(2) points to the Annex VI internal-control route; high-risk status does not automatically mean a notified-body certificate is required. The provider still needs to satisfy applicable requirements. An internal-control route is not permission to omit evidence.

HarborBridge must establish its own lawful and controlled use, qualified oversight, input-data controls, monitoring, records and escalation. Legal must determine any applicable fundamental-rights assessment, data-protection assessment, worker/applicant notices and registration duties; these cannot be inferred solely from a generic checklist. The exact Article 27 applicability analysis remains open and is a release blocker where required.

## Evidence request and follow-through

Procurement requests the provider package within a proposed ten-business-day diligence window. HR and technology prepare local tests; Legal resolves applicability; independent validation determines whether evidence is adequate. The governance office records evidence versions and unresolved gaps. If evidence is unavailable, keep the manual process and evaluate another vendor or a narrower non-ranking use.

Reconsideration requires actual evidence owners, dated review results, resolved critical findings, a defined deployment version and authorized acceptance. This portfolio demonstrates the reasoning needed to make that decision; it does not claim the underlying product exists or passed review.
